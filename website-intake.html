YOUR FIRST WEBSITE DEMO · ABOUT 5–10 MINUTES

A few answers.
A clearer starting point.

Short replies are enough. “Not sure” is useful too. Your answers stay in this page until you copy, download, or send them yourself.

01

Business name, what you do, and where you serve customers. Local, remote, or both?

02

Your ideal customer, what they need help with, and the 1–3 services to feature.

03

Choose a main action: request a quote, book, call, message, buy, or other. Who receives requests, and what happens next?

04

Up to three essentials: service details, prices or quote process, common questions, or examples of your work.

05

Your logo, colors, approved photos, or 1–2 websites you like. No assets yet? Describe the style.

06

Your current site and any email, calendar, booking, customer/job management, or payment tools to keep.

07

Target date. Optional: setup budget and comfortable monthly cost. “Not sure” is welcome.

08

Mention payments, customer sign-in, private documents, sensitive information, or anything to keep off the site. Share no private records.

No passwords, card details, or private customer records. Nothing is uploaded. Refreshing this page clears your answers.
