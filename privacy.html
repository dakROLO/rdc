TEST WEBSITE PRIVACY

A clear view of
your information.

This notice describes this redesign test site and its browser-only workspace preview.

Website questionnaire

The questionnaire prepares a summary in your current browser tab. Answers are not automatically uploaded or saved to local storage. Refreshing or closing the page clears them. Downloading saves a file to your device; copying uses your clipboard. Opening an email draft does not send it: you must choose Send in your email app.

Workspace preview

The workspace uses sample project data. Actions and choices change this tab only. Exporting a review downloads a file to your device. There is no connection to live customer records, payment services, or portal sign-in in this preview.

External services

If you follow a booking or email link, that service handles the information you provide under its own terms and privacy practices. Contact RDC if you need help choosing a way to share information.

Hosting and contact

The hosting provider may process technical request information to operate and protect the site. This test release adds no analytics or advertising scripts. If you send an email, RDC receives the information you choose to include for responding and discussing the work. Ask Dakota about access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent.

Share only what is needed

Do not enter passwords, card details, sensitive documents, or private customer records. Uploads and protected customer work require a separately agreed secure process.

Questions: dakota@royaldigitalclarity.com.
