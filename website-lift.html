COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we start.

Do I need to know which platform I want?

No. We begin with your offer, customer journey, current tools, and editing needs. Then we recommend the smallest dependable architecture.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We inspect the current site and ownership first, preserve useful content and URLs, and plan redirects and cutover where needed.

Can the site connect to booking or payments?

Yes, when included in the scope. We select the provider and test the actual confirmation, follow-up, and recovery process.

Will I own the domain and accounts?

Our default is customer-controlled business accounts, with code and content rights documented in the agreement. We establish the handoff before provisioning.

Is the first questionnaire a project commitment?

No. It supplies the direction for discovery and an initial demo. We agree scope, costs, and publication separately.
